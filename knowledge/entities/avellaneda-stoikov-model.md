# Avellaneda–Stoikov model

## What it is
Brownian mid-price, Poisson market orders reaching a quote at distance $\delta$ with intensity
$\Lambda e^{-k\delta}$, CARA utility over a finite horizon; solved approximately (proposal p. 12).
Source not yet acquired (`avellaneda-2008-high-frequency-trading`).

## In the core sources
[[martin-2026-market-making-hawkes]] keeps $p=e^{-\kappa\delta}$ but makes κ a self-excited state
(pp. 4–5); [[acero-2026-hawkes-structured-networks]] says its model «extends» AS with Hawkes market
orders (p. 2).
